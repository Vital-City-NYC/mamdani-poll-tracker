How to read this

Three questions appear here and they are not interchangeable. Job approval asks whether the respondent approves or disapproves of how the mayor is handling his job. A performance rating asks for a grade of excellent, good, fair or poor; this page counts excellent plus good as positive, and fair is not disapproval. Favorability asks about the person rather than the job and usually runs higher than approval.

Who is asked and how matters too. All adults, registered voters and likely voters answer differently. Suffolk polls by live phone; Marist and Emerson mix phone, text and online panels. Emerson offers a neutral option, which pulls both approve and disapprove down, so the margin between the two is a steadier guide than either number alone.

Included: named pollsters that publish field dates, sample size and question wording. Excluded: campaign or interest-group polls released without crosstabs, and straw polls.
