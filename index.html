How to read this

Three questions appear here and they are not interchangeable. Job approval asks whether the respondent approves or disapproves of how the mayor is handling his job. A performance rating asks for a grade of excellent, good, fair or poor; this page counts excellent plus good as positive, and fair is not disapproval. Favorability asks about the person rather than the job and usually runs higher than approval.

Populations and methods differ too. All adults, registered voters and likely voters answer differently. Suffolk polls by live phone; Marist and Emerson mix phone, text and online panels. Emerson offers a neutral option, which pulls both approve and disapprove down.

Included: named pollsters that publish field dates, sample size and question wording. Excluded: campaign or interest-group polls released without crosstabs, and straw polls. The previous-mayor comparison uses each mayor's first-year polls placed by months since inauguration, with approve-or-disapprove readings drawn as circles and excellent-or-good grades as squares; hover a point for the pollster and who was surveyed.
