# Bill de Blasio, first 12 months: job-approval polls (fielded Jan–Dec 2014)

Inaugurated January 1, 2014. Citywide polls only, read from the pollster's own pages. Compiled October 2, 2026. Machine-readable version: `deblasio.json`.

Quinnipiac's "New York City voters" are registered voters (RV). Quinnipiac PDFs on poll.qu.edu were fetched directly and the text extracted locally; the Q1 table (with DK/NA) and the "From [dates], Quinnipiac University surveyed N New York City voters" line were checked in each one. The December 18, 2014 trend table lists the whole year's series and matches every entry below.

## Verified

### 1. Quinnipiac, Jan 9–15, 2014, RV, n=1,288: approve 53, disapprove 13, unsure 34
URL: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/nyc/nyc01162014_cb3td4.pdf (released Jan 16, 2014; fetched direct)
> "Voters approve 53 – 13 percent of the job Mayor de Blasio has done so far, with 34 percent undecided, the independent Quinnipiac (KWIN-uh-pe-ack) University poll finds."
> "From January 9 – 15, Quinnipiac University surveyed 1,288 New York City voters, with a margin of error of +/- 2.7 percentage points."

### 2. Marist (WSJ/NBC 4 New York), Feb 27–Mar 4, 2014, RV, n=586: excellent 10, good 29, fair 37, poor 20, unsure 5 (excellent+good 39)
Type: performance (grade question), registered voters only. 785 adults interviewed in total; the job-rating table is published for RV only.
URL: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/37-new-mayor-but-city-still-divides/ (released Mar 7, 2014; fetched direct)
Tables PDF: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/wp-content/misc/nycpolls/c140227/de%20Blasio/Complete%20March%207,%202014%20The%20Wall%20Street%20Journal_NBC%204%20New%20York_Marist%20Poll%20NYC%20Release%20and%20Tables.pdf
> "39% of registered voters in New York City approve of the job Mayor de Blasio is doing in office. This includes 10% who think he is doing an excellent job and 29% who say he is doing a good one. 37% rate de Blasio's performance as fair while one in five voters -- 20% -- thinks he is performing poorly. Five percent have either never heard of de Blasio or are unsure how to rate him."
> "This survey of 785 New York City adults was conducted February 27th through March 4th, 2014 by The Marist Poll sponsored in partnership with The Wall Street Journal and NBC 4 New York. [...] There are 586 registered voters. Results for this subset are statistically significant within ±4.0 percentage points."

## Method notes
- web.archive.org was not reachable from the fetch tool, so no archived copies were used; everything above is a live pollster page.
- Quinnipiac HTML release pages exist at poll.qu.edu/poll-release?releaseid=N (2050 = June 11, 2120 = Dec 18); the other 2014 IDs were not located, so the PDF is the URL of record for Jan, Mar, Aug and Nov. The older Poll-Release-Legacy and release-detail URL forms return only navigation.
- Correction to the task's known leads: the June 2014 Quinnipiac reading is 51–28, not 50; 50–32 is August 26.
