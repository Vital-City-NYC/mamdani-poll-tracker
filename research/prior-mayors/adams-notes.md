# Eric Adams, first 12 months: job-approval polls (fielded Jan–Dec 2022)

Citywide polls only, read from the pollster's own pages. Compiled Oct 2, 2026. Companion file: adams.json.

## Verified

### Quinnipiac University, Feb 3–7, 2022, 1,343 RV. Approve 46, disapprove 27, no opinion 27
- Release: https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?ReleaseID=3834 (released Feb 9, 2022)
- PDF: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/nyc/nyc02092022_nywx73.pdf
- Quote: "With roughly five weeks on the job as New York City mayor, voters approve 46 – 27 percent of the job Adams is doing so far with 27 percent not offering an opinion." Methodology: "1,343 New York City registered voters were surveyed from February 3rd – 7th with a margin of error of +/- 2.7 percentage points."
- Fetched direct.

### Marist Poll, March 1–8, 2022, 891 adults (745 RV). Adults: approve 61, disapprove 24, unsure 15. RV: approve 62, disapprove 25, unsure 13
- Release: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/marist-new-york-city-poll-mayor-eric-adams-job-performance-march-2022/ (published March 14, 2022; no media sponsor named)
- Tables PDF: https://maristpoll.marist.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Marist-Poll_NYC-NOS-and-Tables_202203091244.pdf
- Quote (release page, adults): "Many New York City residents (61%) approve of the job Eric Adams is doing as mayor. 24% disapprove." "A notable 15% are unsure." "38% of city residents, including 40% of registered voters, think Adams is an excellent (9%) or good (29%) mayor."
- RV figures come from table ADAE105 in the PDF (row order: NYC Adults 61/24/15, NYC Registered Voters 62/25/13). Performance grade in the same poll: adults 9 excellent, 29 good, 37 fair, 14 poor, 10 unsure; RV 9/31/37/14/9.
- Fetched direct (page via WebFetch; curl is blocked by Cloudflare).

### Quinnipiac University, April 28–May 2, 2022, 1,249 RV. Approve 43, disapprove 37, no opinion 20
- Release: https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?ReleaseID=3845 (released May 4, 2022)
- PDF: https://poll.qu.edu/images/polling/nyc/nyc05042022_nydg85.pdf
- Quote: "Voters give Mayor Eric Adams a 43 – 37 percent job approval rating with 20 percent not offering an opinion. In Quinnipiac's previous New York City poll released on February 9, 2022, voters gave Adams a 46 – 27 percent job approval rating with 27 percent not offering an opinion." Methodology: "1,249 New York City registered voters were surveyed from April 28th – May 2nd with a margin of error of +/- 2.8 percentage points."
- Fetched direct.

### Spectrum News NY1 / Siena College, May 22–June 1, 2022, 1,000 adults. Performance grade: excellent/good 29, fair/poor 64 (no approve/disapprove question)
- Release: https://sri.siena.edu/2022/06/07/big-apple-is-sour-on-the-job-mayor-adams-is-doing-only-29-rate-his-job-performance-positively-but-53-like-his-style/ (released June 7, 2022)
- PDF: https://scri.siena.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Spectrum-NYC-6-7-22-PR-Final.pdf
- Quote: "While 29% of New York City adults think Mayor Eric Adams is doing an excellent or good job as mayor, 64% say he's doing only a fair or poor job." The 53–37 figure in the headline is about the mayor's "style", not job approval.
- Fetched direct.

## Unverified or excluded

- Quinnipiac "summer 2022": no such poll. Quinnipiac's trend tables list only Feb 9 and May 4, 2022, then Feb 1, 2023.
- Siena, Jan 9–13, 2022 (Adams 63% excellent/good among NYC voters): statewide poll, 806 RV statewide; NYC figure is a subsample. Excluded.
- Emerson/PIX11/The Hill, May and Aug 2022: NY-10 and NY-12 district samples, not citywide. Excluded.
- Slingshot Strategies "24% deserve a second term" (1,424 voters, via Politico): June 2024, not 2022. No 2022 Slingshot citywide poll found.
- Data for Progress: no 2022 NYC Adams approval poll found; earliest is Sept 2024.
- City & State/Honan, May 2022: "political insiders," not a public sample.
- No other Marist or Siena city-only poll in 2022 was found.
