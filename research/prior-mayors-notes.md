# Prior mayors: first-year job ratings

Compiled 2026-10-02 for the Mamdani poll tracker overlay. Companion to `prior-mayors.json`. Every entry below lists the URL the numbers were read from and a verbatim quote. Months since inauguration = field-period midpoint minus January 1 inauguration, in days divided by 30.4375.

Metric key: **approve/disapprove** = a direct approve-or-disapprove question. **excellent/good** = the four-point excellent, good, fair, poor scale used by Marist and by Siena in 2022. In the JSON the 'approve' field for those rows holds excellent+good and 'disapprove' holds fair+poor.

## Comparability

**Quinnipiac** (Bloomberg 2002, de Blasio 2014, Adams 2022) asked the same question across all three eras: "Do you approve or disapprove of the way [name] is handling his job as mayor?" Registered voters, live-interviewer phone. Bloomberg 2002 and de Blasio 2014 releases describe the sample as "New York City registered voters" or "New York City voters." This is the cleanest series for an overlay. Quinnipiac itself made this comparison in its March 18, 2014 release: "A March 27, 2002, survey showed newly-elected Mayor Michael Bloomberg with a 62 - 16 percent approval rating" (https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?releaseid=2021).

**Marist** used the excellent, good, fair, poor scale for decades and calls excellent+good an "approval rating." It is not an approve/disapprove question: "fair" is a middling answer that gets lumped with "poor," so these numbers run well below approve/disapprove readings. The same Marist survey in March 2022 put Adams at 61% approve on the direct question but 40% excellent/good among registered voters. Marist's 2022 trend table is the only long series that reaches Giuliani (Dec. 1994, 51%) and Dinkins (March 1990, 56%, not recorded here because Dinkins is outside the brief). Marist switched to approve/disapprove for New York City by 2022 and used that question for both Adams (2022) and Mamdani (2026).

**Siena** asked approve/disapprove for the New York Times in 2014 (adults) and excellent/good/fair/poor for Spectrum News NY1 in 2022 (residents). Its 29% for Adams in June 2022 is on the excellent/good scale and should not sit on the same line as Quinnipiac's 43% approve.

**Populations differ.** Quinnipiac = registered voters. Marist 2022 and 2026 approval = all adults (Marist's RV subset ran a point higher for Adams, 62/25/13). Marist excellent/good series = registered voters. Siena = adults/residents, with RV figures in notes where published.

**Undecided share shrinks over the first year.** Bloomberg's first Quinnipiac reading was 65-6 with 29% undecided; de Blasio's was 53-13 with 34% undecided. Net approval (approve minus disapprove) is more comparable across early polls than raw approve.

**Recommendation for the chart:** plot one approve/disapprove series per mayor (Quinnipiac plus Siena 2014 plus Marist 2022/2026 approve), and either drop the excellent/good rows or show them as a separate, clearly labeled Marist-scale view. Giuliani has only an excellent/good point, at month 11.4.

## Gaps (searched, not recorded)

- CBS News/New York Times New York City polls of June 4-8 2002 (ICPSR 3697), August 2002 (ICPSR 3703) and June 1994 (ICPSR 6598) asked about Bloomberg's and Giuliani's performance, but ICPSR returned 403 and no secondary source quoting the toplines was found. Not recorded.
- No Quinnipiac New York City polls exist for 1994 in Quinnipiac's online archive (release IDs scanned for 2001-2004 and 2014; no 1994 NYC releases found). No Daily News or NYT 1994 Giuliani approval number was found online.
- Marist's Bloomberg trend table goes straight from March 2002 to April 2004; its de Blasio trend goes from March 2014 to May 2015. So Marist has one first-year reading for each.
- Siena's January 2022 poll (Jan. 9-13) gave Adams +43 net favorability in its New York City subsample of a statewide registered-voter poll. That is favorability, not job approval, and was excluded. Some outlets misdescribe it as a 63% approval.
- Quinnipiac did not poll Adams between May 2022 and February 2023 (its trend table: Feb 9 2022, May 4 2022, Feb 1 2023). No other public Adams job-approval poll from June to December 2022 was found.
- Siena's April 2014 release for the Times and NY1 could not be fetched (Cloudflare block). Its 49-31 figure is confirmed by Siena's December 2014 release.
