- YouGov public-figure tracker. National, not NYC. Excluded.
- Washington Examiner (Aug 12, 2026) cites a "56%-34%" April figure. That is the Siena April NYC subsample, not a separate poll.

The page lists pollsters such as "Meridian Research," "Vantage Analytics," "Northgate Insights" and "Harborview Data" with mechanically increasing sample sizes and identical margins. It looks like placeholder or synthetic data. Do not use.
